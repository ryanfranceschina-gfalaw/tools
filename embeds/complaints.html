Section H: Terms of Submission

Please scroll and read the Terms of Submission in full. The acknowledgment box will become available once you have reached the end.

1. Scope

These Terms of Submission ("Terms") govern the submission, intake, docketing, and review of grievances concerning statements or opinions expressed by attorneys of GFA LAW, PLLC (the "Firm") through the Office of Grievance Intake (the "Office"). By submitting a grievance, the complainant ("Complainant") agrees to be bound by these Terms in their entirety.

2. Definitions

"Statement" means any oral, written, or electronically published expression attributed to an attorney of the Firm. "Grievance" means a complaint submitted through this portal concerning a Statement. "Docketing" means the assignment of a case number and the entry of the Grievance into the Office's review log. "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or Firm-observed holiday.

3. Eligibility and Timeliness

A Grievance must be submitted within seventy-two (72) hours of the occurrence giving rise to it. The Office may, in its sole discretion, decline to docket any Grievance that is untimely, incomplete, duplicative of a prior submission, or that does not identify a specific Statement. A determination that a Grievance is not eligible for docketing is final.

4. Submission Requirements

All mandatory fields must be completed. The Complainant is responsible for the accuracy and completeness of all information submitted. The Office will not correct, supplement, or interpret a submission on the Complainant's behalf. Supplemental materials may be requested by the Office and must be provided within five (5) Business Days of the request.

5. Review Process

Docketed Grievances are assigned to a reviewer in the order received. The reviewer will evaluate the Grievance under the standards set forth in the Firm's internal policies, which are not published and are subject to change without notice. The Complainant is not entitled to participate in the review, to receive interim updates, or to be informed of the identity of the reviewer.

6. Timelines

The Office does not commit to any particular timeline for review. Estimated processing times displayed in this portal are provided for convenience only and do not create any obligation on the part of the Office or the Firm.

7. Retention

Grievance records are retained in accordance with the Firm's document retention schedule. The Complainant acknowledges that the retention period applicable to a given Grievance is determined by the Firm and is not disclosed. Records may be disposed of at the conclusion of the applicable retention period without notice to the Complainant.

8. Confidentiality

Information submitted through this portal will be handled in accordance with the Firm's internal confidentiality practices. The Office does not guarantee confidentiality with respect to any Statement that was itself public at the time it was made. Submission of a Grievance does not create an attorney-client relationship with the Firm or any of its attorneys.

9. Communications

All communications regarding a Grievance must be directed through this portal. The Office does not respond to inquiries submitted by email, telephone, mail, or social media. The Complainant agrees not to contact individual attorneys of the Firm regarding a pending Grievance.

10. Resolution

Any resolution offered is determined at the sole discretion of the Firm. The resolution options presented in Section C of this portal are provided for classification purposes only and do not constitute an offer, promise, or representation that any particular resolution is available.

11. Appeals

A Complainant who disagrees with the disposition of a Grievance may request reconsideration in accordance with the Firm's internal procedures. Requests for reconsideration are reviewed by the Office of Appeals. The Office of Appeals is not obligated to respond to any request.

12. No Waiver

Nothing in these Terms, and nothing in the acceptance, docketing, review, or disposition of any Grievance, shall be construed as an admission by the Firm or any attorney of the Firm, or as a waiver of any right, privilege, or defense.

13. Governing Law

These Terms are governed by the laws of the State of Texas without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Any dispute arising from these Terms shall be resolved exclusively through the procedures described herein.

14. Acknowledgment

By checking the acknowledgment box and initialing below, the Complainant affirms that they have read these Terms in full, understand them, and agree to be bound by them.
