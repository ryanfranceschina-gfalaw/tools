What We Do

Core Practice Areas

Innocent DefenseWe are trial attorneys. Innocent Defense is how we practice criminal defense: we prepare every case for trial, hold the State to its burden of proof beyond a reasonable doubt, and fight to clear your name. Civil Rights LitigationHolding law enforcement and government officials accountable when they violate your constitutional rights, through federal lawsuits under 42 U.S.C. § 1983. First Amendment DefenseThe First Amendment is the cornerstone of a free society, and it is increasingly under attack from government overreach and administrative censorship. We aggressively defend your right to speak and assemble. Second Amendment DefenseThe right to keep and bear arms is a fundamental liberty. Our firm challenges unconstitutional restrictions on law-abiding gun owners in Texas.
