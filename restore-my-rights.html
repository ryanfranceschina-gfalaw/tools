Restore My Rights: Federal Firearm Rights Restoration

GFA LAW, PLLC helps clients apply to the Department of Justice to restore federal firearm rights under 18 U.S.C. 925(c).

About the DOJ program
  • The program: 18 U.S.C. 925(c); 28 C.F.R. Part 107 (final rule, 91 FR 54054, Aug. 20, 2026; effective Sept. 21, 2026). It is administered by the DOJ Office of the Pardon Attorney and covers all 18 U.S.C. 922(g) disabilities, not just felony convictions.
  • Timing: public applications opened Nov. 4, 2026 for the first 5,000 applicants. A second window opens Jan. 4, 2027 for 500 more, when the $30 DOJ fee begins.
  • Fingerprints: by USPS electronic fingerprinting only.
  • What relief does: it removes federal disabilities only. State firearm law and voting rights are untouched, and relief is discretionary, not guaranteed.

What the application asks for
  • Your personal information, including your Social Security number. Enter it only in our secure application form, never by email or text.
  • Every address where you have lived in the last 25 years, or since age 18 if that is shorter.
  • Details for each conviction or status that affects your firearm rights: case number, court, dates, and sentence.
  • Three character references who are not related to you, have known you for at least three years, and are not prohibited from having firearms.
  • A flat $350 consultation once we review your application. Paying for a consultation does not guarantee the firm will take your case.
  • Documents go by email to restoremyrights@gfalaw.us with the subject line Restore My Rights Documents - Last Name, First Name. The application tells you which ones.
