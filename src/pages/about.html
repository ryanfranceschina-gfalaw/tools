The Partnership

About GFA LAW, PLLC

GFA LAW is led by CJ Grisham, the firm’s Senior Partner, together with Managing Partner Ryan Franceschina. Born from a shared commitment to the Bill of Rights and our Constitution, our firm operates as a unified team to protect citizens from government overreach through aggressive civil litigation and dedicated Innocent Defense.

Our office is at 4970 E. Business 190 in Temple, Texas. We represent clients in Temple, Killeen, Belton and across Bell County, and throughout Texas, in civil rights lawsuits under 42 U.S.C. Section 1983, First and Second Amendment matters, firearm recovery and expunctions, and criminal defense from municipal court through felony trials. Ryan Franceschina is licensed in Texas and Maryland.

CJ Grisham

Senior Partner | The Texas 2A Attorney

{{pic:/assets/img/headshot-cj-grisham.jpg|CJ Grisham, the Senior Partner of GFA LAW, PLLC|800|800|card-img}}

CJ Grisham is the Senior Partner of GFA LAW and leads the firm. Known across the state as The Texas 2A Attorney, he is a fierce defender of the Second Amendment and a nightmare for government entities that overstep their bounds. As the founder of Open Carry Texas, he has spent over a decade on the front lines of constitutional advocacy.

CJ’s legal work is backed by a career of real-world service. A retired U.S. Army First Sergeant and decorated combat veteran, his background in military intelligence brings a tactical edge to the courtroom. He litigates complex civil rights lawsuits under 42 U.S.C. § 1983, including the qualified immunity defenses that government officials raise in them.

A graduate of the Appalachian School of Law, CJ combines veteran discipline with an uncompromising commitment to the original intent of the Constitution.

Ryan James Franceschina

Managing Partner | Innocent Defense

Licensed in both Texas and Maryland, Ryan brings a multi-jurisdictional perspective to the firm’s fight against state overreach.

Ryan earned his Juris Doctor at the Appalachian School of Law, where he and CJ first aligned their principles on constitutional law and conservative values.
