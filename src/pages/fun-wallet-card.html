Free Tool

Know Your Rights Wallet Card

Under stress, it is easy to forget what to say. Make a credit-card-sized card with the key phrases, add an emergency contact, and keep it behind your license.

Nothing you type leaves your device. The card is built in your browser and is not saved or sent anywhere.

Print at 100% scale (not "fit to page"), cut out both sides, and fold or glue them back to back.

This card is general information, not legal advice. Showing it does not replace saying the words out loud: courts have held that you must clearly state that you are invoking your rights. Read more in our guide to police stops and arrests in Texas.
