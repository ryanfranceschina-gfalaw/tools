What this is, and what it is not

It is

  • Written legal analysis from a licensed attorney, researched on professional legal databases
  • Available for matters governed by Texas or Maryland law, and federal law in those states
  • Confidential

It is not

  • A meeting, phone call or ongoing representation
  • Someone appearing in court, filing for you, or contacting the other side
  • Deadline monitoring. If anything is due within 14 days, hire a lawyer now: we do not accept those matters for written review

Common questions

Who reviews my matter?

A licensed GFA LAW attorney reviews and signs every memo. Texas matters are reviewed by a Texas-licensed attorney; Maryland matters by our Maryland-licensed attorney. We use secure research and AI-assisted drafting tools, and an attorney checks every word before it goes to you.

Where does my payment go?

Your payment is held in our client trust account and is earned only when we send your memo. If we cannot take your matter, or we decline it before starting work, you get a full refund.

Can GFA LAW take my case afterward?

Sometimes. The engagement ends when we send your memo, and any further work needs a new written agreement. If you hire us on the same matter within 30 days, we credit what you paid toward that engagement.

What matters do you not review?

We may decline matters outside Texas and Maryland, matters with a conflict of interest, matters with a deadline in the next 14 days, or subjects outside our practice. If we decline, we refund your payment in full.

Does this make GFA LAW my lawyer?

Only for the limited written review described in your signed agreement. Submitting a request does not create an attorney-client relationship.
