Get the most out of your request

  • Explain why you need an attorney. For example: "I was arrested for unlawful carry and need help defending my Second Amendment rights." Avoid vague phrases like "help with my criminal case."
  • Include the key facts: what happened, approximate dates, the county, any case or cause number, the officers or agencies involved, and any important evidence or documents.
  • Fill out every required field (name, phone, email) accurately.
  • Clear, complete requests are reviewed first.

We keep what you submit confidential. Until we confirm we can help, please share only the basic facts. If your matter fits our practice areas, a team member will contact you about next steps. Requesting a case review is free. If we can help, the consultation is $350. Submitting a request does not create an attorney-client relationship.
