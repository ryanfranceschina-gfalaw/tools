GFA LAW on the Radio

Ryan Franceschina and CJ Grisham are frequent guests on Hi-Cap Adventures Radio (Aaron's Gun Shop Radio), hosted by Matt Betros on KMIL 105.1 FM out of Cameron, Texas. The show covers gun laws and safety, the Texas License to Carry, disaster preparation and local news, with new shows every Saturday.
