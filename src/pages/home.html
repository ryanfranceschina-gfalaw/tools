Who We Are

The Constitution is a shield for every citizen

At GFA LAW, PLLC, we believe the Constitution is a shield for every citizen. Our firm is dedicated to the aggressive protection of fundamental American liberties. When the government overreaches, we stand in the gap to ensure your voice is heard and your rights are respected.

From our office in Temple, we represent clients in Bell County and across Texas in civil rights lawsuits, First and Second Amendment matters, and criminal trials.

How a case review works

Request a case review

Use the civil or criminal case review form, or call the office. Give us a short summary of what happened, the dates involved, and any upcoming court settings. Requesting a case review is free.

Consultation

If we can help, we contact you for more information or to schedule a consultation. We receive more requests than we can review, so not every request receives a response. If we can help, the consultation is a flat $350.
