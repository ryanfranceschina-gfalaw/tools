Free Tool

The Stop

Red and blue lights fill your mirror. Make your choices one at a time and see what Texas and federal law say about each one. Score 14 out of 14 to earn a perfect stop.

The Stop is a simplified training game, not legal advice. Every encounter is different, and officers do not always follow the law. Never physically resist; challenge an unlawful stop or search later in court. Learn more in our guides to police stops and arrests and carrying a handgun.
