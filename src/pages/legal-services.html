Good to know

What you get

  • Work done and signed by a licensed GFA LAW attorney, researched on professional legal databases
  • Matters governed by Texas or Maryland law, and federal law in those states
  • Confidential, delivered in writing through your secure portal
  • A flat fee quoted up front. If your documents run longer than the page limit, we quote the difference before any work begins

What it does not include

  • Meetings, phone calls or ongoing representation
  • Appearing in court, filing for you, negotiating, or contacting the other side for you (except a letter we send on letterhead as part of your service)
  • Deadline monitoring. If anything is due within 14 days, call 254-405-1726 or hire a lawyer now: we do not accept those matters as flat-fee services

Does this make GFA LAW my lawyer?

Only for the limited service described in your signed agreement. The engagement ends when we deliver your work, and any further help needs a new written agreement. Submitting a request does not create an attorney-client relationship.

What if you cannot take my matter?

We may decline matters outside Texas and Maryland, matters with a conflict of interest, matters with a deadline in the next 14 days, or matters we are not the right fit for. If we decline, you get a full refund.
