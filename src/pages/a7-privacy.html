The Secret Free Speech Platform

Privacy

This notice adds to the GFA LAW Privacy Policy and covers only this board.

What we keep

When you join, we store the username and profile picture you choose, a coded version of a random number your browser creates (there are no passwords or accounts), and the time you confirmed you are 18 or older, promised to keep the platform secret, and confirmed The Only Rule. We also store your posts, your thumbs up and thumbs down votes, and the time of each. This is kept in a Google Sheet that GFA LAW controls.

Deleting your posts

You can delete your own posts from the same browser you posted them from. A deleted post leaves the board right away, but we keep a copy, with the time you deleted it, in our records. We do this because the law can require us to preserve certain posts, and so we can answer legal requests.

What is public

Your username, picture and posts are public to anyone who visits this board. Vote totals are public; who voted is not.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email or contact details, and we do not store your IP address. Google, which runs the service that receives your posts, may record technical information such as your IP address under its own policies. The rest of gfalaw.us uses Google Analytics as described in the main Privacy Policy.

Your browser

Your browser keeps the random number, your username and your votes so the board remembers you. Clearing your browser's site data removes them, and you will start over with a new username.

Review, records and the law

We review posts to remove unlawful content only as described in The Only Rule. We keep posts and our review notes in our records even after a post is hidden. If the law requires it, such as a report of child exploitation to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children, or a valid subpoena or court order, we may share posts and related records with the authorities.

Adults only

This board is only for adults. If we learn that a user is under 18, we will block that user and hide their posts.
