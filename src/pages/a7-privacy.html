The Secret GFA Group Chat

Privacy

This notice adds to the GFA LAW Privacy Policy and covers only this group chat.

What we keep

When you join, we store the username, password (in scrambled form) and profile picture you choose, a coded version of a random number each of your browsers creates, and the time you confirmed you are 18 or older, promised not to leak the group chat, and confirmed The Only Rule. We also store your messages, which message each reply answers, your emoji reactions (including when you remove one), past usernames, and the time of each. This is kept in a Google Sheet that GFA LAW controls.

Deleting your messages

You can delete your own messages. A deleted message leaves the chat right away, but we keep a copy, with the time you deleted it, in our records. We do this because the law can require us to preserve certain messages, and so we can answer legal requests.

What members see

The chat is walled off: only signed-in members can read it. Every member sees your current username, picture, messages and reaction totals. Who reacted is not shown. When you change your name, your old messages show the new name.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email or contact details, and we do not store your IP address. Google, which runs the service that receives your messages, may record technical information such as your IP address under its own policies. The rest of gfalaw.us uses Google Analytics as described in the main Privacy Policy.

Your browser

Your browser keeps the random number and your username so the chat remembers you. Signing out or clearing your browser's site data removes them; sign back in with your username and password.

Passwords

A password is required. It lets you sign in with the same username on other devices. We never store your password itself, only a scrambled, one-way version of it that no one, including GFA LAW, can turn back into your password. Even so, this platform is not secure: please use a new and unique password that you do not use anywhere else. There is no password reset. We limit repeated sign-in attempts to protect accounts.

Review, records and the law

We review messages to remove unlawful content only as described in The Only Rule. We keep messages and our review notes in our records even after a message is hidden. If the law requires it, such as a report of child exploitation to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children, or a valid subpoena or court order, we may share messages and related records with the authorities.

Adults only

This chat is only for adults. If we learn that a member is under 18, we will block that member and hide their messages.
