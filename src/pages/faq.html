Frequently Asked Questions

Don't see your question? Call 254-405-1726 or email intake@gfalaw.us.

How much does a consultation cost?

Requesting a case review is free. If we can help, a consultation with GFA LAW is a flat $350. You pay the fee after we confirm your consultation is scheduled. Your payment is deposited into our client trust account and is not earned until your consultation takes place. Paying the consultation fee does not mean GFA LAW has agreed to represent you.

How do I request a case review?

Use the civil or criminal case review form on our Contact page, or call the office. Give us a short summary of what happened, the dates involved, and any upcoming court settings.

What happens after I submit a request?

Our team reviews each request and contacts you if we need more information or can schedule a consultation. If you have a court date or deadline approaching, tell us in your request so we can see it right away.

Do you take cases outside Temple?

Yes. Our office is in Temple, and we represent clients in Bell County and across Texas. Ryan Franceschina is also licensed in Maryland and handles Maryland matters.

Do you take plea deals?

We are trial attorneys. Innocent Defense means we prepare every case for trial from the start. Any decision about how your case resolves belongs to you, and we will explain your options so you can make it with full information.

How are your fees set?

Fees depend on the type of case and the work involved. If we take your case, your fee arrangement is explained in a written engagement agreement before work begins.

What should I bring to the consultation?
  • Citations, arrest paperwork, bond papers and court notices
  • Any letters or documents from the government or the other side
  • Photos, videos and the names of witnesses
  • A short timeline of what happened
Is what I tell you confidential?

Yes. What you share with us while we review your request is kept confidential, even if we do not end up representing you. Until we confirm we can help, please share only the basic facts and do not send sensitive documents until we ask for them. An attorney-client relationship with GFA LAW begins only after you sign an engagement agreement and the fee is received.

Where can I get GFA LAW merch?

Visit our Merch page or our Etsy shop.

This page is general information and is not legal advice for your situation.
