The Secret Free Speech Platform

There are no house rules

This is free speech in its purest form. Say anything. The only posts that come down are the ones the law requires us to take down. Nothing else.

The one promise: keep it secret

This is not a content rule; it is the price of admission. The platform is a tight circle that spreads by word of mouth only. Never post about it, link to it, screenshot it, or mention it anywhere public. Tell only people you trust to keep it quiet, and teach them the same. Be careful who you let in.

What stays up

Everything the law protects. Rude, offensive, unpopular, crude, wrong, angry, political, religious, irreverent, critical of the government, critical of us. We will not remove a post because we dislike it, disagree with it, or find it offensive. There is no "community standard," no viewpoint filter, and no algorithm. Newest posts show first, for everyone.

What the law requires us to take down

That is the whole list:

  • True threats. A serious expression of intent to commit unlawful violence against a particular person or group. Jokes, hyperbole and heated political talk are protected and stay up.
  • Child sexual exploitation. Federal law requires us to report it to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children.
  • Soliciting a specific crime. Offering or asking someone to commit a particular crime, like selling illegal drugs or hiring someone to hurt a person. Arguing that a law is wrong, or should be broken or changed, is protected and stays up.
  • Sex trafficking. Federal law does not protect a site that knowingly hosts it.
  • Unlawful doxxing. Posting someone's home address, phone number or similar personal information to threaten, harass or retaliate against them, including against public servants, judges, officers and their families.
  • Copyright infringement a copyright owner properly reports to us (see below).
  • False testimonials about GFA LAW. As a law firm, the lawyer advertising rules forbid us from hosting false or misleading statements about our own services or results. Criticism of the firm is welcome and stays up.

If we are unsure whether a post crosses one of those lines, it stays up while an attorney reviews it. Hidden posts are kept in our records, not deleted.

The fine print

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • Everything you post is public. You can delete your own posts from the board, from the same browser you posted them from. Deleted posts leave the board, but we keep a copy in our records.
  • Posts are the opinions of the people who write them, not GFA LAW, PLLC.
  • Nothing here is legal advice, and posting does not create an attorney-client relationship. Do not post about your own legal matter; if you need help, request a case review.
  • You are responsible for what you post. Speech that is unlawful anywhere else is unlawful here too.
  • If someone floods the board with spam or bots, we may pause posting for everyone until it stops. That is about the machinery, never the message.

Reporting a post

If you believe the law requires us to remove a post, email info@gfalaw.us with the username, the post and the date, and tell us which legal line it crosses. "I don't like it" is not one of them.
