Quick Legal Primer: What Builds (or Breaks) a §1983 Claim

  • Foundation: Government actor under color of law — no claim without this.
  • Violation: Clear Fourth Amendment (excessive force, unlawful arrest/search) or First Amendment (retaliation) breach.
  • Evidence & Harm: Video/bodycam, witnesses, medical records — these win cases. No provable damages = limited recovery.
  • Liability: Patterns or prior complaints help sue the department (Monell) and fight qualified immunity.
  • Time Limit: In Texas, most §1983 claims must be filed within **2 years** of the incident (personal injury SOL borrowed). Exceptions are rare.

Qualified Immunity Reality: Even strong cases often face this defense. Only skilled attorneys can argue the right was "clearly established."

Important Disclaimer: This educational tool uses general principles — NOT legal advice. It cannot predict outcomes. §1983 cases are highly fact-specific, evidence-dependent, and frequently defended aggressively. Small details matter enormously.
