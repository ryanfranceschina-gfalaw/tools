Tools

Free tools from the trial attorneys at GFA LAW to help people in Texas and Maryland understand their rights, track their deadlines and gather the evidence that matters. Every tool has a Texas and a Maryland version. Each tool is general information, not legal advice, and any date a tool gives you is an estimate to confirm with a lawyer.

Can the Cop Do That?

A quiz on traffic stops, searches, ID laws, recording police and carrying a handgun.

Deadline Checker

Estimate the filing and notice deadlines for civil rights suits, claims against state and local governments, federal tort claims, criminal appeals and whistleblower claims. Results are estimates only. Exceptions and tolling can change your real deadline, so confirm it with a lawyer.

Case Review Finder

Answer a few questions and get sent to the right case review form, or told to call now if it cannot wait.

Records Request Builder

Write a Texas or Maryland Public Information Act request for bodycam video, police reports and 911 calls.

Firearm Return and Expunction Checker

See whether you may be able to get a seized firearm back or clear an old arrest from your record.

Know Your Rights Guide

What to do and say during a stop, a search or an arrest, with a printable guide.

Restore My Rights

Screening for the Department of Justice federal firearm rights restoration program under 18 U.S.C. 925(c).
