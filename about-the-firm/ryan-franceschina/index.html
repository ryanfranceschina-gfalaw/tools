Ryan Franceschina

Managing Partner, GFA LAW, PLLC | Temple, Texas

Ryan Franceschina is the Managing Partner of GFA LAW, PLLC (Grisham Franceschina Attorneys-at-Law, PLLC). He handles civil rights lawsuits, constitutional litigation and criminal defense, and he runs the firm's day-to-day operations from its Temple office, serving clients in Bell County and across Texas.

Practice Focus

  • Civil rights lawsuits under 42 U.S.C. § 1983 against officers, agencies and municipalities
  • First and Second Amendment litigation
  • Criminal defense, including misdemeanor and felony matters
  • Firearm recovery and expunctions

How Ryan Works With Clients

Ryan prepares every criminal case as if it will be tried and builds civil rights cases on the record: body-camera and dash-camera video, reports, policies and witness accounts. Clients get a clear explanation of their options, and every decision about how a case resolves stays with the client.

Education and Bar Admissions

  • J.D., Appalachian School of Law (2022)
  • Licensed to practice law in Maryland (2023), Maryland Bar No. 2305250023
  • Licensed to practice law in Texas (2025), State Bar of Texas No. 24151119
  • U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Texas
